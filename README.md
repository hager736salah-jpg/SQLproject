# Adidas Sales & Product Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI report analyzing Adidas sales performance, profitability, regional/city distribution, and product/channel breakdown across two pages (`SALES` and `PRODUCT`).

## Demo Video
Watch the walkthrough video here: [https://youtu.be/8pjrOY7ko6A]

## Key Highlights from the Report
- **Sales & Profitability (`SALES` page):** Tracks Total Sales (`182.08M`), Total Quantity (`462K`), Operating Profit (`63.38M`), and Operating Margin (`40.37%`) along with MoM changes, yearly/monthly trends, regional & operating profit breakdown, and top cities.
- **Product & Sales Channels (`PRODUCT` page):** Breaks down Total Sales & Operating Profit by Product, Operating Margin by Product, Average Operating Margin by Sales Method (`Online`, `Outlet`, `In-store`), Quantity by Region, Quantity by Sales Method, and Total Sales by Sales Method.

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Data modeling & visual reporting (`Data Sales Adidas`, `CALENDER`)
